Grade 6 Fractions Unit: Lesson Plan and Schedule

Posted by Ms. Priya Raman, Head of Mathematics, on 1 August 2026

This four-week unit builds understanding of fractions, decimals and percents before procedures. Students first model fractions with strips, number lines and area diagrams, and only then learn the symbolic rules. The unit follows NCERT Class 6, Chapter 7, and the Karnataka state syllabus.

Learning objectives

  • Explain why fractions need a common denominator before they can be added or subtracted.
  • Compare fractions with unlike denominators using benchmarks such as 1/2 and 1.
  • Convert between fractions, decimals and percents, for example 3/4 = 0.75 = 75%.
  • Multiply and divide fractions and explain why dividing by 1/2 doubles a number.

Common misconceptions we will address

Adding numerators and adding denominators (1/2 + 1/3 = 2/5); believing a larger denominator means a larger fraction; thinking a longer decimal such as 0.125 is bigger than 0.5; and adding successive percentage discounts. Each misconception gets a diagnostic question at the start of the lesson.
